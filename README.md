# CrossCheck

Proactive cross-document contradiction detection. Point it at a corpus of policies,
contracts, specs, or regulations and it surfaces every pair of statements that conflict —
with evidence quotes, source citations, a classified contradiction type, and a calibrated
confidence score.

> Under active development. The full quickstart, results table, calibration plot, and demo
> live here once the pipeline and evaluation harness are complete (see the build plan in
> `CLAUDE.md`).
