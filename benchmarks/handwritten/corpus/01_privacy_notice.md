# Vantis Privacy Notice

Version 3.0. Published by Vantis Data Ltd, Dublin. This notice is written for the people
who use the Vantis platform, and we have tried to keep it in plain language.

## 1. About this notice

This notice, version 3.0, replaces the version we published in March 2024 and takes effect
on 1 September 2025. Earlier versions are archived on our website for reference only and no
longer describe how we work. We review this notice at least once a year, and we will tell
you by email before any change that materially affects you. Where we say "your
information", we mean anything that identifies you or your organisation, including the
contents of your workspace, the tickets you raise with us, and the records we keep about
your account. If you have a written agreement with us, that agreement and its addenda set
out the legal detail; this notice explains it in everyday terms.

## 2. What we do with your information

We use your information to run the platform, to answer your questions, and to keep your
account secure. Only you can see the contents of your workspace. We do not share your
information with any third party for their own purposes. We do share it with the companies
that help us operate — hosting, payment processing, email delivery — and every one of them
is listed on our trust centre before we start using them. Our support team, which is based
in Manila and Bangalore, may open your workspace when that is the only way to resolve a
ticket you have raised. Signing in requires multi-factor authentication, and we will not
turn that requirement off on request.

## 3. Where we keep your information, and for how long

Your information is stored in the region you select when you sign up, and you can see which
region that is at any time on your account page. We keep the records we hold about your
account for two years after you close it, so that we can deal with billing questions and
disputes. Support tickets and their attachments are deleted after 12 months. Nothing we
hold about you is kept indefinitely; every category of information we collect has an end
date, and when it is reached the information is removed. Backups are taken every 24 hours
and are covered by the same rules.

## 4. Your choices

You can download a copy of everything we hold about you at any time from the account page,
and you can ask us to correct anything that is wrong. We answer requests about your
information within ten days. You can close your account yourself without contacting us
first. As of version 3.0 we no longer embed third-party analytics scripts on the Vantis
console, so nothing you do inside the product is measured by an outside company. We still
use a first-party cookie to keep you signed in, and you can clear it at any time by signing
out. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to us
first and then to your national data protection authority.
