# Data Processing Addendum

This Addendum forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement between Vantis Data Ltd
("Vantis") and the customer identified in the Order Form ("Customer").

## 1. Definitions and precedence

"Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural
person that Vantis Processes on behalf of Customer under the Agreement. "Sub-processor"
means any third party engaged by Vantis to Process Personal Data. "Security Incident" means
a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration,
unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Personal Data. "Business Day" means a day other
than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Ireland. This Addendum takes effect on 1 June
2025 and prevails over any conflicting term in the Trust and Security Overview or in any
other Vantis policy, standard or marketing document, whenever published.

## 2. Roles and Sub-processors

Vantis acts as Processor and Customer acts as Controller. Vantis shall Process Personal
Data only on documented instructions from Customer, including with regard to transfers, and
shall not Process Personal Data for its own purposes or those of any third party. Vantis
shall give Customer at least 30 days' prior written notice before appointing a new
Sub-processor, and shall maintain a current list of Sub-processors at its trust centre.
Customer may object to a proposed Sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds, and
Vantis shall not onboard that Sub-processor for so long as the objection remains
outstanding. Vantis remains liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors.

## 3. Security, incidents and audit

Vantis shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including
encryption of Personal Data at rest using AES-256 and an independent penetration test
carried out at least once a year. Vantis shall notify Customer of a Security Incident
without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, and shall
provide sufficient information for Customer to meet its own notification obligations.
Customer may audit Vantis' processing facilities and records once per calendar year, on 30
days' prior written notice, either itself or through an independent auditor appointed by
it. Vantis shall make available all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate
compliance with this Addendum.

## 4. Transfers, data subject requests and deletion

Vantis shall not transfer Personal Data outside the European Economic Area, nor permit
access to it from outside the European Economic Area, except where the transfer is covered
by the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission. Taking into account
the nature of the Processing, Vantis shall assist Customer in responding to a request from a
data subject to exercise their rights within ten Business Days of receiving the request.
On expiry or termination of the Agreement Vantis shall, at Customer's election, return or
delete all Personal Data, and shall complete that deletion within 30 days of termination.
