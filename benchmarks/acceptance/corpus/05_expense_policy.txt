ARDEN SYSTEMS
TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Version 3.0 - Issued by Finance - Effective 1 March 2026

1. SCOPE

This policy governs reimbursable business expenses incurred by Arden Systems personnel. It
applies to employees, and to contractors where their statement of work provides for expense
reimbursement. Finance administers this policy and approves exceptions.

2. GENERAL PRINCIPLES

Personnel may incur reasonable business expenses in the ordinary course of their duties.
Expenses must be necessary, proportionate, and supported by documentation. Personal
expenses must never be charged to a company card.

Receipts are required for any expense over 75 US dollars. Expenses below that threshold may
be claimed with a description alone. Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the
expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.

3. AIR TRAVEL

Economy class is the standard for all flights under six hours. Premium economy may be
booked for flights over six hours without prior approval. Business class requires advance
approval from a vice president. First class is not reimbursable under any circumstance.

Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance where the travel date is known.
Personnel may retain frequent flyer benefits earned on business travel.

4. ACCOMMODATION

Hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to 250 US dollars per night in listed high-cost
cities and up to 180 US dollars per night elsewhere. The current high-cost city list is
maintained by Finance. Room service is reimbursable as a meal expense.

5. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT

Meals during business travel are reimbursable up to 80 US dollars per day. Alcohol is a
reimbursable expense when incurred as part of a client meal. Client entertainment over 500
US dollars requires advance approval from a department head.

6. GROUND TRANSPORT

Taxi and ride-hailing fares are reimbursable for business journeys. Personnel using a
private vehicle for business travel are reimbursed at the published mileage rate. Parking
and toll charges are reimbursable with a receipt. Traffic fines are never reimbursable.

7. COMPANY CARDS

Company cards are issued to personnel who travel more than four times a year. Cardholders
must reconcile transactions within 30 days of the statement date. Repeated failure to
reconcile results in withdrawal of the card.

8. APPROVAL AND AUDIT

Line managers approve expense claims for their direct reports. Finance audits a random
sample of 5 percent of claims each quarter. Personnel must retain original documentation
for 24 months after submission.

9. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Financial Officer.
Approved exceptions are recorded in the finance exception register.
