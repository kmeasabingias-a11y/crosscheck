# Arden Systems — Paid Time Off Policy, Version 2

Effective 1 January 2026. Issued by People Operations. This policy supersedes Section 2 of
the Employee Handbook, Revision 4.0.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the paid time off entitlement for Arden Systems personnel. It replaces
the paid time off provisions previously published in the Employee Handbook. Where this
policy and the Employee Handbook differ, this policy controls from its effective date.

## 2. Entitlement

All full-time employees receive 25 paid time off days per calendar year. Part-time
employees accrue paid time off on a pro-rata basis according to contracted hours. Employees
in their first year of service accrue entitlement monthly from their start date.

Paid time off is available for use immediately on joining. The 90-day waiting period
previously applied to new joiners is withdrawn.

## 3. Carry-Over

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused paid time off days into the following calendar
year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March or they are forfeited. Carry-over
beyond 5 days requires written approval from a vice president.

## 4. Requesting Time Off

Employees must request paid time off at least 14 days in advance through the People
Operations portal. Requests of more than 10 consecutive days require manager approval at
least 30 days in advance. Managers may decline a request only where the absence would leave
a team without adequate coverage.

## 5. Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from paid time off and is not capped. Employees must notify their
manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for any
absence exceeding three consecutive working days.

## 6. Public Holidays

Public holidays are additional to the paid time off entitlement and follow the published
holiday calendar for the employee's country of employment. Employees required to work on a
public holiday receive a day off in lieu.

## 7. Payment on Termination

Accrued unused paid time off is paid out on termination at the employee's final base rate.
Carried-over days from the previous year are included in this calculation. Negative
balances are deducted from final pay.

## 8. Contractors

This policy does not apply to independent contractors. Contractor entitlements are governed
by the Contractor Engagement Addendum.

## 9. Review

People Operations reviews this policy annually. The next scheduled review is January 2027.
