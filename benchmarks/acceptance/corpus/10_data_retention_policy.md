# Arden Systems — Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Version 2.4. Issued by Legal and the Data Protection Office. Applies to all systems that
process Arden Systems or customer data.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long Arden Systems retains each category of data and how data is
disposed of at the end of its retention period. Every system owner is accountable for
applying the schedule in Section 3 to the systems they own.

## 2. Principles

Data is retained only for as long as it serves a documented business or legal purpose.
Retention periods run from the date the record was created unless the schedule states
otherwise. Deletion is permanent and is confirmed by the system owner in the retention
register.

Personal data must not be retained beyond its scheduled period merely because storage is
inexpensive. Where a legal hold applies, the hold overrides the schedule until Legal
releases it.

## 3. Retention Schedule

Customer contract records are retained for seven years after the contract terminates.
Financial records are retained for seven years after the end of the financial year.
Employee personnel files are retained for six years after employment ends. Recruitment
records for unsuccessful candidates are retained for 12 months.

Application and access logs are retained for 13 months. Security incident records are
retained for five years after the incident is closed. Backup media is retained for 90 days
on a rolling basis. Marketing contact records are retained for 24 months after the last
recorded interaction.

## 4. Customer Data on Termination

Customer production data is deleted within 30 days of contract termination unless the
customer requests an export. Where an export is requested, the data is retained for a
further 30 days and then deleted. Customer data held in backups is removed on the ordinary
backup expiry cycle.

## 5. Deletion Methods

Electronic records are deleted using the platform's supported deletion facility. Physical
media is destroyed by an approved contractor who issues a certificate of destruction. Paper
records are shredded on site to a cross-cut standard.

## 6. Legal Hold

Legal issues a hold notice to system owners when litigation is reasonably anticipated. Data
under hold must not be deleted, altered, or moved. System owners confirm receipt of a hold
notice within five working days.

## 7. Exceptions and Review

Exceptions to the retention schedule require written approval from the Data Protection
Officer. The Data Protection Office reviews this policy every 12 months and after any
material change to applicable law.
