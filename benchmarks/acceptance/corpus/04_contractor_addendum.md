# Arden Systems — Contractor Engagement Addendum

Version 2.1. Issued by Legal and People Operations. This addendum governs the engagement of
independent contractors by Arden Systems, Inc. and its subsidiaries.

## 1. Status

Independent contractors are not employees of Arden Systems. Contractors are responsible for
their own taxes, social contributions, and insurance. Nothing in this addendum creates an
employment relationship.

## 2. Engagement Terms

Every contractor engagement requires a signed statement of work before work begins. The
statement of work must specify the deliverables, the rate, and the engagement end date.
Engagements are capped at 12 months and may be renewed once with Legal approval.

## 3. Benefits and Leave

Contractors engaged for more than six months are entitled to paid time off on the same
pro-rata basis as part-time employees. Contractors accrue this entitlement from the start of
month seven of the engagement. Paid time off for contractors is invoiced at the contracted
day rate.

Contractors are not eligible for company health insurance. Contractors are not eligible for
the annual bonus scheme or for equity awards.

## 4. Rates and Invoicing

Contractors invoice monthly in arrears. Arden Systems pays undisputed contractor invoices
within 30 days of receipt. Invoices submitted more than 90 days after the work was performed
may be rejected. Rate increases require a new statement of work.

## 5. Equipment and Access

Contractors use their own equipment unless the statement of work provides otherwise.
Contractors granted corporate network access must complete security onboarding before
access is issued. Contractor accounts are disabled automatically on the engagement end date.

## 6. Intellectual Property

All work product created by a contractor in the course of an engagement is assigned to
Arden Systems on creation. Contractors must not incorporate third-party code into a
deliverable without written approval from Legal. Contractors retain no licence to
deliverables after the engagement ends.

## 7. Confidentiality

Contractors must sign the standard mutual non-disclosure agreement before receiving any
confidential information. Confidentiality obligations survive the end of the engagement by
five years.

## 8. Insurance

Contractors providing services on company premises must carry professional indemnity
insurance of at least 1 million US dollars. Evidence of cover must be provided to Legal
before work begins.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate an engagement on 14 days written notice. Arden Systems may
terminate immediately for breach of the confidentiality or intellectual property terms.
Contractors must return all company property within seven days of termination.
