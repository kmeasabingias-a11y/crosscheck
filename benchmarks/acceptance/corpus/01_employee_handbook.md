# Arden Systems — Employee Handbook

Revision 4.0. Issued by People Operations. Applies to all Arden Systems personnel
worldwide unless a country-specific addendum states otherwise.

## 1. Scope and Application

This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Arden Systems, Inc.
Independent contractors are governed by their individual statements of work and by the
Contractor Engagement Addendum. Where this handbook conflicts with a written employment
agreement, the employment agreement controls.

## 2. Paid Time Off

All full-time employees receive 20 paid time off days per calendar year. Part-time
employees accrue paid time off on a pro-rata basis according to contracted hours.
Contractors are not entitled to paid time off. Paid time off accrues monthly and becomes
available for use after the employee completes 90 days of continuous service.

Unused paid time off does not carry over into the following calendar year. Employees must
request paid time off at least 14 days in advance through the People Operations portal.
Managers may decline a request only where the absence would leave a team without adequate
coverage. Paid time off is paid out on termination at the employee's final base rate.

## 3. Health Insurance

The company provides health insurance to all full-time employees. Coverage begins on the
first day of employment. Employees must enroll within 30 days of their start date.
Part-time employees working at least 25 hours per week are eligible for health insurance
at a subsidised rate. Dependants may be added during the annual enrollment window.

## 4. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:30 local time, Monday through Friday. Employees are
entitled to a 60-minute unpaid meal break each working day. Overtime must be approved in
writing by a department head before it is worked. Non-exempt employees are paid overtime
at 1.5 times their base hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a week.

## 5. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Remote work
from outside the employee's country of employment requires written approval from both
People Operations and the Legal department. Employees working remotely must use a
company-issued device and must connect through the corporate VPN.

## 6. Business Expenses

Employees may incur reasonable business expenses in the ordinary course of their duties.
Receipts are required for any expense over 25 US dollars. Expense claims must be submitted
within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Air travel in premium cabins requires
advance approval from a vice president. Alcohol is not a reimbursable expense.

## 7. Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential company information to any third party during or
after their employment. Confidential information includes customer lists, pricing, source
code, and unreleased product plans. Employees must return all company property and
confidential materials on their last day of employment.

## 8. Termination

Employees who resign are asked to provide four weeks of written notice. The company will
provide statutory notice or payment in lieu where employment is terminated without cause.
Final pay, including accrued unused paid time off, is issued within 14 days of the
termination date.

## 9. Amendment

People Operations reviews this handbook annually. Amendments take effect on the date
published in the People Operations portal. This revision remains in force until it is
formally withdrawn.
