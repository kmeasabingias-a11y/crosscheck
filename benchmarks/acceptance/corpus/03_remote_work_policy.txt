ARDEN SYSTEMS
REMOTE AND DISTRIBUTED WORK POLICY
Version 1.3 - Issued by People Operations and Workplace Services

1. PURPOSE

Arden Systems supports distributed work where the role permits it. This policy sets out
where and how personnel may work outside a company office. It applies to all employees and
to contractors who have been granted corporate network access.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Any employee whose role does not require physical presence may work remotely on a full-time
basis. Employees may work remotely five days per week without restriction. Managers may not
require attendance at an office except for scheduled team events, which are limited to four
per year.

Roles requiring physical presence include hardware laboratory work, facilities management,
and reception. Personnel in those roles are not eligible for remote work.

3. LOCATION

Employees may work from any location worldwide provided they have a reliable internet
connection. Employees who relocate must notify People Operations within 30 days of the
move so that payroll and tax withholding can be adjusted.

Work from a location outside the employee's country of employment for more than 30
consecutive days may create a tax presence for the company and must be logged in the
People Operations portal.

4. EQUIPMENT

The company issues a laptop to every remote worker. Employees may use a personal device for
company work provided the device has full-disk encryption enabled and an approved endpoint
agent installed. The company contributes up to 500 US dollars towards home office equipment
in an employee's first year.

Company-issued equipment remains company property and must be returned within 14 days of
the termination date.

5. CONNECTIVITY AND SECURITY

Remote workers must connect to company systems through the corporate VPN whenever they
access internal services. Public wireless networks may be used provided the VPN is active.
Employees must lock their screen whenever they leave their workstation unattended.

Personnel must not print documents classified as Confidential or Restricted outside a
company office.

6. WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY

Remote workers keep the standard working hours set out in the Employee Handbook. Employees
must be reachable on the company messaging platform during core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 in
their local time zone. Meetings are scheduled to respect the published core hours of all
participants.

7. EXPENSES

Home internet costs are not reimbursable. Coworking space costs are reimbursable up to 200
US dollars per month with manager approval. Travel from a remote worker's home to a company
office is not reimbursable except for scheduled team events.

8. REVIEW

Workplace Services reviews this policy every 12 months. Questions about eligibility should
be directed to People Operations.
